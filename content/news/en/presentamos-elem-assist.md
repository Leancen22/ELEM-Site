Today we are launching **ELEM Assist**, an AI assistant for your company's website. We built it for the questions that arrive every day: how long shipping takes, how to return a product, where to pick up an order. The answers are already published somewhere, but they are scattered across pages, PDFs and emails.

## What it does

It is added to your site with a single line of code and shows up as a chat button. It answers from what the company loaded into its knowledge base: documents, notes, images and the site's own pages. Every fact comes with a citation, so the person asking can see where it came from. When useful, it shows an image (a store map, for example) or offers a form to download. If it can't find the information, it says so instead of making it up.

It also knows which page the person is on. Visitors can highlight a paragraph and ask about it, and the answer takes into account both the highlighted text and the knowledge base.

## The knowledge base

You upload PDF, Word, Excel or image files, or write notes. Each document gets a summary, tags and the questions it answers, with no manual work, and images are described so they show up in searches too. Documents are linked to each other in a graph that shows what was used for each answer. If your site runs on Drupal, its pages and files are synced automatically.

## Guided flows

For processes that always follow the same steps, such as checking an order, there are guided assistants that don't use AI and have no per-query cost. They are built on a canvas with options and questions that are validated (for example, an ID number with its check digit). They can query the company's systems and save the answers.

## Control and privacy

The dashboard shows what people ask, which topics come up again and again, and which questions went unanswered, so you know what content is missing. Every conversation can be reviewed, and a corrected answer becomes the official one. When needed, the conversation is handed over to someone on the team. Search runs on models hosted on your own server, and sensitive parts of a page can be excluded so the assistant doesn't read them.
