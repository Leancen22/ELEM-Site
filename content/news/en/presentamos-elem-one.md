Today we are launching **ELEM One**, an intranet and HR platform for companies. We built it so that internal communication and everyday HR processes no longer live scattered across email, spreadsheets and messaging groups.

## What it includes

The foundation is internal communication. There is a feed for announcements and updates, a news section that can be targeted to a department or location, direct and group chat, surveys, and live streams for all-hands meetings. Anyone who misses a stream can watch the recording later.

On the HR side there are time-off and leave requests, time tracking, payslips, and a portal where each person opens their requests and can see where they stand. HR stops answering the same question by email ten times and keeps everything recorded in one place.

There are also internal courses with assessments and certificates, performance reviews and departmental objectives. They cover onboarding for new hires as well as ongoing training for the rest of the team.

Finally, it includes an assistant that answers questions about company policies and procedures. It only uses what the company has published for the whole team and has no access to personal data.

## How it adapts

Each company turns on the modules it will use and leaves the rest off. It works in the browser and on mobile. Access can require two-step verification or use the corporate accounts the company already has, and every administrative change is logged.
